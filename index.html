My Last Letter to You

To the girl who once made my world a little more beautiful,

I don't really know how to say goodbye to someone who has meant so much to me. There are so many things I wish I could explain, so many words that might never be enough to describe what you've meant to my heart.

Thank you for the smiles, the conversations, the little moments, and the memories that I will always carry with me. Maybe some of those moments seemed ordinary to you, but to me, they became something special.

I'll miss our playful moments, our laughter, and all the times we spent together. I didn't make this decision simply because I wanted to, but because I thought it would be for the best. I know it would be difficult for you to balance your feelings, and you might get confused having two people in your heart.

If this is where our story ends, I hope you know that meeting you was never something I would regret. You became a beautiful part of my life, and I will always be grateful that, for a little while, our paths crossed.

let me pls just love you from afar until this feelings of mine fades. Leaving you was for your own good is my final act of love for you. Don't worry about me or where I am right now. I'll be okay, I'll try my best to get through this. All I can do now is pray that you two will be happy together. I hope you get to fulfill all the fantasies and dreams you've always wanted to experience together.

I loved you more than you could ever imagine. I know that the world doesn't revolve around happiness alone, but I always pray that you'll keep smiling and that you'll always be happy.

I know you won't lose anything if I ever disappear from your life. You might even feel much lighter because there will only be one person on your mind. As for me, I don't know how I'm going to heal or how I'll find the strength to get back up again. I've grown so used to having you by my side.

I'm learning that sometimes loving someone also means accepting that we cannot keep every person we love beside us. It hurts, but not every beautiful thing is meant to last forever in the same way. If you ever find yourself remembering me, don't hesitate to reach out and talk to me. You can always open up to me whenever you feel like you have no one on your side.

I hope life treats you gently. I hope you achieve your dreams, find reasons to smile on your hardest days, and never forget how deserving you are of genuine happiness.

And if someday you happen to remember me, I hope it isn't only with sadness. I hope you remember the laughter, the memories we shared, and the person I was when I was happy to have you in my life.

This isn't me asking you to look back. It's simply me saying thank you for being part of my story. No matter where life takes us, what we shared will always have its own little place in my memories.

So, this is my goodbye. Not because everything we shared meant nothing, but because it meant something to me. And some things deserve a gentle ending.

I'm happy that I pursued you and tried to win your heart,
I Love You So Much, Yen. 🌸
